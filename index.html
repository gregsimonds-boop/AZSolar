A better first conversation

Good questions. Clearer choices.

A little preparation makes it easier to compare solar proposals.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your ZIP code, utility company, recent electricity bills, roof age, and whether you own the property. Mention existing solar and any battery interest.

Will solar eliminate my electricity bill?

Ask for a written explanation of expected production and any remaining utility charges. Results depend on your system, use, rate plan, and other property-specific factors. No savings or bill outcome is guaranteed here.

What should I ask about battery backup?

Ask which circuits the proposed system can support, for how long, and whether backup equipment is included. Have the provider explain the specific equipment and operating limits.

How do I compare payment options?

Compare total cost, ownership, payment changes, maintenance responsibilities, warranties, and what happens if you sell your home. Have the provider explain every agreement before you sign.

Which communities does this guide cover?

Our geographic focus includes Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria, Goodyear, Avondale, and nearby communities. A provider must confirm availability for your ZIP code; this list is not a coverage guarantee.

Is Arizona Sun Power the installer?

No. Arizona Sun Power is an independent information and referral resource. We do not design, install, finance, or service solar systems. Providers are independent businesses, and we may receive compensation for qualifying calls.
